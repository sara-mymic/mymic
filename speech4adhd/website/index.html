Three ways to practice

Pick what fits the moment: solo speaking, a back-and-forth with Buddy, or a playful agree/disagree challenge.

🎙️

My Voice

Get a friendly prompt, record your answer, listen back, and hear Buddy repeat—your words echoed clearly so you can hear how you sound and try again.

💬

Let’s Chat

Have a relaxed back-and-forth with Buddy using the big mic. Short, encouraging replies keep the conversation flowing—perfect for warming up your speaking.

⚡

Quick Debate

Pick a side on a fun topic, say why, and Buddy responds with a kind, playful reply. A low-stakes way to practice making your point out loud.

Why we built it this way

Speaking practice works best when it feels safe, short, and a little fun.

  • Large tap targets and readable type—easy to use without squinting.
  • Short turns so energy stays high and you don’t feel put on the spot.
  • Positive framing: celebrate trying, not “perfect” speech.
  • Friendly, everyday topics—no pressure, no grades.

Who it’s for

myMic is for anyone who wants to practice speaking and build confidence—language learners rehearsing out loud, people preparing for conversations or interviews, and kids who do better with clear structure and positive feedback (including kids with ADHD). It’s a practice tool, not therapy or medical care.

If a child uses myMic, we recommend a parent or guardian set it up and review the privacy details together. See our Privacy Policy for how recordings and messages are handled.
